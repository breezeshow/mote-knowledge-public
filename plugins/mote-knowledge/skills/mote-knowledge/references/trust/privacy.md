# Privacy

Mote's user-facing privacy posture. The authoritative source for any specific
claim is [mote.com/privacy](https://www.mote.com/privacy); this file consolidates
the practical facts an agent or RFP response needs.

## Core commitments

Three commitments Mote makes plainly on mote.com/privacy:

- **"We never sell your data."**
- **"We never advertise in Mote."**
- **"We don't own the content you add to Mote."**

## What Mote collects

**From all users:** first and last name, email address, profile photo (via
Google SSO if available), school or organization name, user role (teacher /
student), age indicator (over / under 18), voice recordings, text content, IP
address, browser and device information, language information, and usage
analytics.

**From paying customers:** billing and credit-card information for payment
processing.

**Mote does *not* collect:** date of birth, student ID numbers, home addresses,
parent/guardian email (unless voluntarily given to support), grades or academic
records, behavioral notes, health information, or social-security / insurance
numbers.

## Retention

- **Inactive accounts:** all personal information and content deleted after
  **one year of inactivity**.
- **Student content:** automatically deleted on the **one-year anniversary** of
  creation, even on active accounts.
- **Active adult accounts:** content retained while the account is active.
- For districts that require it, Mote can extend retention to **5 years
  post-creation + 1 year post-graduation**.

## Cookies

Two categories, both first-party:

- **Essential cookies:** strictly necessary for service operation (registration,
  authentication, age verification).
- **Analytics cookies:** used for product improvement.

**No advertising cookies.** No commercial exploitation of cookie data.

## Data-subject rights

Users may access their personal information, request correction, request
deletion, object to processing, and request data portability. Requests are
processed within 30 days via `support@mote.com`.
