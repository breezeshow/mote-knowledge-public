# Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

**Universal Design for Learning** is an educational framework, developed by CAST,
for designing lessons that work for the full range of learners from the start,
rather than retro-fitting accommodations for a few. Its premise: learner variability
is the norm, so learning should offer **multiple means** of engaging with,
perceiving, and acting on it. UDL has three principles, each with three guidelines.
(Mote follows CAST's UDL 3.0 guidelines.)

## Mote's UDL coverage

Mote publishes its alignment to the framework at
[mote.com/udl/mote-alignment-with-udl](https://www.mote.com/udl/mote-alignment-with-udl):
a feature-by-consideration map. Mote covers **18 of the 36 UDL considerations
(50%)**, unevenly by principle:

| Principle | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Action & Expression | 64% |
| Representation | 58% |
| Engagement | 31% |

That shape is honest and worth keeping to: Mote is an accessibility *toolkit*, so it
serves the principles about perceiving and expressing learning far more than the one
about motivation. The sections below break the mapping down guideline by guideline;
the live page is the authoritative source for the exact feature-to-consideration map.

## Representation: multiple means of representation

*How information is presented; the "what" of learning.*

- **Guideline 1 · Perception (2/3).**
  [Read Aloud](../product/features/read-aloud.md) carries this: human-like voices,
  adjustable speed, and reading text inside images, so a learner can perceive
  content by ear as well as by sight.
- **Guideline 2 · Language & Symbols (5/5, full coverage).** Mote's strongest
  guideline. The [Dictionary](../product/features/dictionary.md) (including the
  multilingual and picture dictionary, and Personal Vocabulary) clarifies
  vocabulary; [Webpage Translation](../product/features/webpage-translation.md),
  live and simultaneous across languages, opens content up; Read Aloud's diverse
  voices and its Google Forms support round it out.
- **Guideline 3 · Building Knowledge (0/4).** No Mote features. This guideline is
  about connecting prior knowledge and constructing meaning, teaching practice
  rather than tooling.

## Action & Expression: multiple means of action and expression

*How learners act on learning and show what they know; the "how." Mote's
highest-coverage principle.*

- **Guideline 4 · Interaction (2/2, full coverage).**
  [Speech to Text](../product/features/speech-to-text.md), the
  [Online Audio Recorder](../product/features/online-audio-recorder.md), and
  [Mote Pages](../product/features/mote-pages.md) give learners varied ways to
  respond and navigate.
- **Guideline 5 · Expression & Communication (3/4).**
  [Text Prediction](../product/features/text-prediction.md) supports the mechanics
  of writing; the [Highlighter](../product/features/highlighter.md)'s notes and
  voice notes offer alternative ways to express understanding.
- **Guideline 6 · Strategy Development (2/5).** The
  [Highlighter](../product/features/highlighter.md) and its labels help a learner
  organize information; the
  [Feedback Workflow](../product/features/feedback-workflow.md) (Loops, Canvas
  SpeedGrader) supports monitoring progress.

## Engagement: multiple means of engagement

*Why learners engage; motivation and persistence. Mote's lightest principle (31%).*

- **Guideline 7 · Welcoming Interests & Identities (2/4).** Flash Cards in
  [Mote Vocab](../product/mote-vocab.md), and engagement tracking.
- **Guideline 8 · Sustaining Effort & Persistence (2/5).**
  [Teacher Feature Controls](../product/platform.md) keep tools in proportion, and
  the [Embedded Voice Recorder](../product/features/embedded-voice-recorder.md) and
  Motebook comment bank support feedback that sustains effort.
- **Guideline 9 · Emotional Capacity (0/4).** No Mote features. This guideline is
  about emotional regulation and self-awareness, outside Mote's scope.

## How to use this file

For a task that must speak to UDL (a district-facing page, an RFP section, a blog
post) name the principle and guideline, then pull the feature files mapped to it.
Mote's honest, strong story is **Representation** and **Action & Expression**; claim
Engagement only as far as choice, autonomy, and feedback genuinely reach. For the
exact feature-by-consideration mapping, cite
[mote.com/udl/mote-alignment-with-udl](https://www.mote.com/udl/mote-alignment-with-udl).
