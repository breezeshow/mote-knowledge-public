# Mote for iOS

**Status: launched.** Mote for iOS is **out of beta and live on the App Store**,
running on iPad and iPhone (announced 2026-08-03). Schools and classes on iPads get
the Mote experience without the Chrome extension. Two entry points worth naming:
**Image Reader** (photograph a worksheet, drag the corners to select a region, and
tap *Read Selected Area* to hear it with follow-along), and **Writer**, which has
word prediction built in. Anything read, scanned or written can be saved to a
**Collection** to return to.

**Description.** Mote for iOS is Mote's native app for Apple devices, listed on the App
Store as **Mote - Read Aloud & Writer**. It is a daily reading and writing companion
for inclusive learning: a personal library where everything a student reads, scans,
records, or writes lives in one place they can return to anytime. A web page, a PDF, a
photo of a page, a voice note, or a piece of their own writing each becomes a library
item that reads aloud, follows along word by word, and resumes where they left off.
The idea is a reading library, not a bag of tools.

**What it includes.**

- **Library and Collections:** every item a student adds becomes a card in a personal
  library, sorted into color-coded Collections by class, project, or mood. Content
  falls into five types: web pages, documents, images, voice notes, and Writer
  documents, and each item resumes from the last word read.
- **Read Aloud:** plays web pages and documents in natural voices, highlighting each
  word as it is spoken, with adjustable speed (0.5x to 2.0x) and a choice of voices.
- **Scan Text:** points the camera at a book, a worksheet, or a sign and pulls the
  text out to read aloud, using on-device recognition first with a cloud fallback,
  and can read just a selected area of a page.
- **Translation:** reads content in another language and speaks it back, built into
  every reader, so a multilingual learner can hear a page in their own language.
- **Voice Recording:** captures a voice note and turns it into clean, replayable text,
  with the words highlighted as the original recording plays back.
- **Speech to Text:** lets a student dictate instead of type, to get past writer's
  block.
- **Writer:** a built-in editor with on-device next-word predictions and read-back of
  the student's own writing.
- **Accessibility:** the OpenDyslexic font, warm reading themes, larger text, and
  read-along highlighting, with appearance controls (theme, font, size, line spacing)
  shared across every reader.
- **Share from anywhere:** send a webpage, PDF, image, or selection to Mote from
  Safari or any app with the iOS Share sheet, and it opens straight into a reader for
  Read Aloud, Translation, and the rest.

**Who it is for.** Students, teachers, and parents, and anyone who wants reading to
feel easier, calmer, or more accessible. Unlike the Chromebook-native Chrome Extension, which
schools and districts deploy centrally, Mote for iOS is an individual app that a person
downloads and signs into on their own device.

**Plans.** Mote for iOS is free to download. The **Writer** offers text prediction for free;
Read Aloud, Scan Text, Voice Recording, dictation, Translation, cloud voices, and
iCloud sync are part of the paid **Mote Unlimited** plan. The app does not currently sell the
plan through the App Store: upgrading requires purchase of a plan via mote.com, and plan 
status is read back from Mote's backend, so an Unlimited or Education plan held elsewhere 
applies here too.

**How it fits with the rest of Mote.** Mote for iOS is the Apple-device counterpart to
the Chromebook-native [Mote Chrome Extension](./chrome-extension.md). Many of the tools are the same in
spirit (Read Aloud, Translation, Speech to Text, and the Writer's predictions and
read-back), but the iOS app is organized around a personal library rather than the Mote
Sidebar that travels the web, and it runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac rather than inside
Chrome.

**Where to find it.** The App Store, as **Mote - Read Aloud & Writer** (Mote
Technologies, Inc.). It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac (Apple silicon), and Apple Vision,
requires iOS 17 or later, and keeps a student's library in sync across their devices
with iCloud. Category: Education. Age rating: 4+.
