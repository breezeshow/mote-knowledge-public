# Mote for iOS

**Status: launched.** Mote for iOS is **out of beta and live on the App Store**,
running on iPad and iPhone (announced 2026-08-03). Schools and classes on iPads get
the Mote experience without the Chrome extension. Two entry points worth naming:
**Image Reader** (photograph a worksheet, drag the corners to select a region, and
tap *Read Selected Area* to hear it with follow-along), and **Writer**, which has
word prediction built in. Anything read, scanned or written can be saved to a
**Collection** to return to. Since 2.1 (November 2026), **Book mode** scans a
chapter or a whole book page by page and saves it as one PDF in the Library (see
[Scanning books and multi-page documents](#scanning-books-and-multi-page-documents)).

**Description.** Mote for iOS is Mote's native app for Apple devices, listed on the App
Store as **Mote - Read Aloud & Writer**. It is a daily reading and writing companion
for inclusive learning: a personal library where everything a student reads, scans,
records, or writes lives in one place they can return to anytime. A web page, a PDF, a
photo of a page, a voice note, or a piece of their own writing each becomes a library
item that reads aloud, follows along word by word, and resumes where they left off.
The idea is a reading library, not a bag of tools.

**What it includes.**

- **Library and Collections:** every item a student adds becomes a card in a personal
  library, sorted into color-coded Collections by class, project, or mood. Content
  falls into five types: web pages, documents, images, voice notes, and Writer
  documents, and each item resumes from the last word read.
- **Read Aloud:** plays web pages and documents in natural voices, highlighting each
  word as it is spoken, with adjustable speed (0.5x to 2.0x) and a choice of voices.
- **Scan Text:** points the camera at a book, a worksheet, or a sign and pulls the
  text out to read aloud, using on-device recognition first with a cloud fallback,
  and can read just a selected area of a page. It scans one page, several pages, or
  a whole book, and a scan of two or more pages is saved as a PDF.
- **Translation:** reads content in another language and speaks it back, built into
  every reader, so a multilingual learner can hear a page in their own language.
- **Voice Recording:** captures a voice note and turns it into clean, replayable text,
  with the words highlighted as the original recording plays back.
- **Speech to Text:** lets a student dictate instead of type, to get past writer's
  block.
- **Writer:** a built-in editor with on-device next-word predictions and read-back of
  the student's own writing.
- **Accessibility:** the OpenDyslexic font, warm reading themes, larger text, and
  read-along highlighting, with appearance controls (theme, font, size, line spacing)
  shared across every reader.
- **Share from anywhere:** send a webpage, PDF, image, or selection to Mote from
  Safari or any app with the iOS Share sheet, and it opens straight into a reader for
  Read Aloud, Translation, and the rest.

**Who it is for.** Students, teachers, and parents, and anyone who wants reading to
feel easier, calmer, or more accessible. Unlike the Chromebook-native Chrome Extension, which
schools and districts deploy centrally, Mote for iOS is an individual app that a person
downloads and signs into on their own device.

**Plans.** Mote for iOS is free to download. The **Writer**'s text prediction and
**Scan Text** (including Book mode and multi-page PDFs) are free; Scan Text is not
part of Unlimited right now. Read Aloud, Voice Recording, dictation, Translation,
cloud voices, and iCloud sync are part of the paid **Mote Unlimited** plan. The app does not currently sell the
plan through the App Store: upgrading requires purchase of a plan via mote.com, and plan 
status is read back from Mote's backend, so an Unlimited or Education plan held elsewhere 
applies here too.

**How it fits with the rest of Mote.** Mote for iOS is the Apple-device counterpart to
the Chromebook-native [Mote Chrome Extension](./chrome-extension.md). Many of the tools are the same in
spirit (Read Aloud, Translation, Speech to Text, and the Writer's predictions and
read-back), but the iOS app is organized around a personal library rather than the Mote
Sidebar that travels the web, and it runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac rather than inside
Chrome.

## Scanning books and multi-page documents

Added in Mote for iOS 2.1.0 and refined in 2.1.1 (November 2026). Announced on
What's New as **"Scan a Book or Handout Straight Into a PDF"** (2026-11-23).

**What it does.** A student with a chapter or a whole book on paper scans it page
by page in the app. The pages are saved as **one PDF in their Library**, ready to
read aloud with word highlighting.

**How it works (the steps in the post):**

1. Open the Mote app and tap **Add**, then **Scan Text**.
2. Choose **Book** and hold the phone or iPad over the first page.
3. Hold steady while it scans. When it says **Turn the page**, turn it. A
   two-page spread is split into two separate pages.
4. Tap **Done**, then **Listen**.
5. The pages open as one PDF and start reading aloud. It's saved in the Library.

**The three scan modes.** The camera offers **Single page**, **Multiple pages**
and **Book**:

- **Single page**: one capture, straight to reading.
- **Multiple pages**: keeps the camera live and collects pages, for a handout or
  worksheet packet.
- **Book**: each shot is an open book's two-page spread, split at the spine
  into two pages. The auto shutter takes each spread once the page is held steady,
  so a student can keep turning pages without tapping.

**Details worth knowing:**

- **Saved as a PDF.** A scan of two or more pages is saved as a PDF in the
  Library's documents and opens in the app's PDF reader. A single-page scan stays an
  image and can be converted to a PDF later (one-way).
- **Add pages later** to a saved scan from its reader.
- **Pages grid:** move, copy, paste, export and delete pages.
- **Reading order is handled for you:** pages come out in reading order, including
  for right-to-left books (such as Arabic or Hebrew). **Settings › Scanning › Book
  page order** overrides it for the rare book Mote gets wrong.
- **Cleaner reading:** two-column pages are read column by column, and page numbers
  and running headers are not read aloud. A page that is only a picture doesn't stop
  the scan becoming a PDF.
- **Stopping a scan keeps the pages already read**, and switching apps doesn't
  interrupt it.
- **Built-in cleanup:** Colour, Greyscale and B&W filters with shadow and lighting
  cleanup.

**How to talk about it.** Lead with the outcome: *a paper book or handout becomes a
PDF that reads aloud*. It is especially useful for print-only class novels,
textbooks and handouts with no digital copy. The PDF lives in the iOS app's
Library and is read there. Don't say it can be opened in Mote PDF (the What's New
post does; don't repeat it). Book mode is an iOS feature. On Chromebooks, Mote PDF's
OCR reads files that are already scanned, but there is no camera scanning.

## Chromebook and iPad compared

| What students get | Chromebook (Mote Chrome Extension) | iPad (Mote for iOS) |
|---|---|---|
| Where it works | On any webpage and inside Google Workspace, through the Mote Sidebar | Inside the app: web pages, PDFs, photos and documents shared into it |
| Read Aloud with word highlighting | Yes | Yes |
| Translation | Yes | Yes |
| Speech to Text | Yes | Yes |
| Word prediction | Text Prediction, wherever they type | In the app's Writer |
| Read a page by taking a photo | No (Mote PDF reads scanned files) | Yes (Scan Text) |
| Scan a book or handout page by page into one PDF | No | Yes (Book and Multiple pages modes) |
| Voice notes | Yes | Yes |
| Read Aloud in Google Forms, voice recording in Docs and Classroom | Yes | No |
| A personal library of everything they read and write | No | Yes (Collections) |
| How it reaches students | IT installs it for everyone from the Google Admin console | Admins can install the app for students, or a student downloads it from the App Store and signs in |

When asked "does it work on iPads?": Mote for iOS brings Read Aloud, Translation
and more to iPad. Don't promise the same tools as on Chromebooks; use this table to
say what differs. Only list iPad tools that appear in this table; which other
tools come to iPad, and which iOS tools are free, is not decided yet. Licensing
works the same way: an Unlimited or school plan
applies in the app too.

**Where to find it.** The App Store, as **Mote - Read Aloud & Writer** (Mote
Technologies, Inc.). It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac (Apple silicon), and Apple Vision,
requires iOS 17 or later, and keeps a student's library in sync across their devices
with iCloud. Category: Education. Age rating: 4+.
