# Mote Writing Review

**Description.** Mote Writing Review checks a student's writing for spelling, homophones
(their / there / they're), syntax, and grammar. Clicking **Review My Writing** takes
the student to Mote Writing Review on their Mote dashboard; it doesn't happen
inline on the page they were writing on. Each flagged issue comes with a
plain-language explainer of *why* it is wrong; there is no "accept" button, so the
student reads the explainer and makes the correction themselves.

**How to use it.** In the Mote Chrome Extension's **Writing Mode**, select the text you've
been writing and click **Review My Writing**. Mote takes you to Mote Writing Review on
your dashboard; click each flagged issue to read its explainer, then make the change
yourself. On a suggestion, **I'll fix it** hands the correction back to the student, and a
confirmation step then checks what they wrote.

Explanations can be played aloud, and the audio **leans on the words being
compared**, so the difference between *their* and *there* is one a student can
hear, not just read.

**Why it exists.** An ordinary spellchecker's red squiggle tells a student that
something is wrong, not *why*, so they fix it and learn nothing. Mote Writing Review
pairs every flagged issue with a plain-language explainer, and it catches
homophone errors that spellcheckers miss entirely. Revision becomes a chance to
understand the pattern, building independence instead of dependence on the tool.

**Where to find it.** The [Mote Chrome Extension](./chrome-extension.md)'s Writing Mode, via the
**Review My Writing** button.
