# Product Overview

**Description.** Mote is the **literacy platform** for reading, writing, speaking,
and listening: in K-12 procurement terms, an **assistive technology software
platform** for students with learning disabilities, reading and writing challenges,
and English Learners, deeply integrated with Chromebooks and Google Workspace. The platform is made up of
six products, with one more that is real but internal.

**On the website** (canonical naming, matching the Webflow Products collection):

- **[Mote Chrome Extension](./chrome-extension.md):** the Chrome extension that opens
  the **Mote Sidebar**, the panel holding the student learning tools (reading,
  writing, translation, audio) organized into Reading, Writing, and Creating modes.
  It also carries the Google Workspace integrations teachers use to make lessons
  accessible.
- **[Mote PDF](./mote-pdf.md):** hosts PDFs and makes them accessible, runs OCR to
  accessibilize scanned files, and adds PDF mark-up on top of the Mote Sidebar
  tools.
  personal reading and writing library.
- **[Mote Vocab](./mote-vocab.md):** vocabulary and study activities, labelled
  **My Vocab** on the customer's dashboard.
- **[Mote Writing Review](./writing-review.md):** spelling, homophone and grammar
  review that explains the rule rather than fixing it. A product because it lives
  in the app; also reachable as a button in the Chrome Extension.
- **[Mote Translator](./translator.md):** a separate product for live translation
  of spoken lessons.

**One internal product** (supported, but not part of the website product set; do
not list it alongside the six above in outward-facing copy):

- **[Mote Platform](./platform.md):** the admin layer for schools and districts:
  accounts, classes, logins, feature controls, and usage data.

**How it fits together.** The Chrome Extension is the core of the everyday student
experience, delivering the Mote Sidebar with its Reading, Writing, and Creating modes; Mote PDF extends it to PDFs, and Mote for iOS brings the reading and writing tools to
iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Mote Platform sits underneath,
letting a school deploy and manage all of it. Mote Vocab turns reading into study
practice, and Mote Translator handles the specific case of live, spoken-lesson
translation.

**Don't-lead-with note.** Mote Translator is an active, supported product, but
should not be proactively surfaced in marketing, sales, or outward-facing copy.
Describe it accurately when a user explicitly asks; lead
with the Chrome Extension and the actively-promoted features otherwise. See
[`brand/terminology.md`](../brand/terminology.md) for the full rule.
