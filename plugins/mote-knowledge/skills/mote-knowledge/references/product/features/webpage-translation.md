# Webpage Translation

**Description.** Webpage Translation lets students translate text on any webpage
into more than 60 languages. Highlight a passage and click the Translate button, and
a pop-up shows the translation, which the student can read, copy, or play aloud in
the chosen language.

**How to use it.** Open the Mote Chrome Extension and go to **Reading Mode**. Highlight the
text you want and click the **Translate** button, or click **Translate** first to
get a click-and-drag tool (like Select Area to Read) and draw a box over the text.
Choose your language; the translation appears in a pop-up, where you can read it,
copy it, or click the play button to hear it read aloud.

**Why it exists.** Multilingual students hit a wall when classroom content is only
in English. Mote translates *any* text on *any* webpage (not just the pages a tool
happens to support) into 60+ languages, and a student can both read the translation
and hear it. It works straight from the Mote Sidebar, so students reach for it
independently, and it lets families engage with school content in their own
language too.

**Where to find it.** The [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md) (Chrome extension).
