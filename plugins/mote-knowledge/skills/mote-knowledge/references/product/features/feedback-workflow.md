# Mote Feedback Workflow

**Description.** The Mote Feedback Workflow gives teachers a sustainable, effective
way to deliver feedback by voice. Recording feedback right inside Google Docs,
Google Classroom, and Canvas SpeedGrader is the fastest way to give high-quality,
personal feedback, and it comes with the tools to make it a habit: **Motebook**, a
comment bank for saving and re-using voice feedback instead of re-recording it;
**Mote Loops**, which let students reply to a teacher's voice note for genuine
two-way conversation; and **Engagement Tracking**, so a teacher can see which notes
have been heard ("Moticed") and how students reacted or replied.

**How to use it.** Look for the Mote icon inside Google Docs comments, Google
Classroom, or Canvas SpeedGrader; click it to record voice feedback on a student's
work, and click again to stop. Students with Mote can play, translate, and reply to
it. Saved feedback lives in your Motebook for re-use, and the dashboard shows
engagement.

**Why it exists.** Written feedback is slow to give and easy for students to skip
past. Mote's voice feedback is faster for teachers and carries the tone and nuance
text can't, and Mote makes it sustainable rather than a one-off: it is built into
the tools teachers already grade in, a Motebook saves comments for re-use, Loops
turn feedback into a conversation, and engagement tracking shows whether it actually
landed. Voice feedback has been shown to support stronger teacher-student feedback
loops (see [`evidence/outcomes.md`](../../evidence/outcomes.md) for the LEO Academy
Trust and Weathers references).

**Where to find it.** The [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md), built into Google Docs,
Google Classroom, and Canvas SpeedGrader.
