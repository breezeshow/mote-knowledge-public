# Text Prediction

**Description.** Text Prediction offers real-time word suggestions as students
type, drawing on phonetic spelling and semantic and contextual awareness, so
suggestions stay relevant even when spelling is approximate. It works in English,
French, and Spanish. Students can load a **topic list** from their vocabulary lists
to give predictions subject-specific context, surfacing the academic vocabulary
needed for, say, a science lab report or a history essay.

**How to use it.** Open the Mote Chrome Extension and go to **Writing Mode**, then select
the text prediction tool. Choose your language, and optionally load a topic list for
context. Set how many suggestions to show, and pick one of three modes:
**Follow** (suggestions appear next to the cursor), **Fixed** (a toolbar along the
bottom of the screen), or **Tap** (nothing appears while typing; the student taps
the icon below their cursor when they want a suggestion). As you type, choose a
suggested word by clicking it or typing its number.

**Tap mode** exists for students who find a list travelling across the page as
they write distracting: suggestions stay hidden until asked for, so support is
available without being present.

**Why it exists.** Writing is where many students stall: on spelling, word-finding,
or the sheer motor effort of typing. Text Prediction keeps them moving. Mote's
version uses phonetic and contextual awareness, so it still helps when spelling is
only approximate; works across English, French, and Spanish; can be tuned to a
subject with topic lists; and sits in the Mote Sidebar alongside the student's other
tools, with no separate app to open.

**Where to find it.** The [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md)'s Writing Mode.
