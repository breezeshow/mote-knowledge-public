# Product Features

One file per feature. Sub-features are woven into their parent feature's file, not
listed separately.

## Chrome Extension
- [Read Aloud](./read-aloud.md): text-to-speech for any webpage, document, PDF, image, or maths.
- [Text Prediction](./text-prediction.md): real-time, context-aware word suggestions as students type. *(also in Writing Mode)*
- [Webpage Translation](./webpage-translation.md): translate webpage text into 60+ languages; read it or hear it aloud.
- [Dictionary](./dictionary.md): multimodal word lookup with images, audio, and native-language definitions.
- [Highlighter](./highlighter.md): color-mark text, with editable labels for reading strategies.
- [Highlight Notes](./highlight-notes.md): a typed note attached to a highlight, recording why it matters.
- [My Annotations](./my-annotations.md): the page where highlights and their notes are stored.
- [Audio Creation](./audio-creation.md): voice recording, text-to-audio, audio upload, and dictate & translate.
- [Screen Mask](./screen-mask.md): a focused single-line reading window.
- [Focus Mode](./focus-mode.md): strips a page back to distraction-free core content.
- [Speech to Text](./speech-to-text.md): dictate text into any active text field.

## Writing Mode (Chrome Extension)
- [Mote Writing Review](../writing-review.md): checks spelling, homophones, and grammar with plain-language explainers.
- Also uses [Text Prediction](./text-prediction.md) and [Speech to Text](./speech-to-text.md).

## Voice messaging and audio (part of the Chrome Extension)
- [Mote Feedback Workflow](./feedback-workflow.md): voice feedback in Docs, Classroom, and Canvas, with Loops, Motebook, and engagement tracking.
- [Embedded Voice Recorder](./embedded-voice-recorder.md): Mote's recorder inside Google Workspace, Gmail, and education apps.
- [Online Audio Recorder](./online-audio-recorder.md): browser-based recorder, no extension needed.
- [Mote Pages](./mote-pages.md): shareable, automatically transcribed audio pages.
