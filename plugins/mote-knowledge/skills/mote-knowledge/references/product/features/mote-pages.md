# Mote Pages

**Description.** A Mote Page is a universally accessible web page for sharing a
short-form audio recording. Each page comes with automatic transcription and is
shared through a single link or QR code, so anyone can listen, with no extension or
account needed.

**How to use it.** Record a Mote, then share its Mote Page link or QR code.
Whoever opens it can play the audio and read the transcript.

**Why it exists.** Sharing a quick audio clip usually means a file to send, an app
to open, or an account to create. A Mote Page is just a web link (or a QR code)
that anyone can open on any device, with an automatic transcript included. A
recording reaches students, families, or colleagues with no friction, and stays
accessible to anyone who would rather read than listen.

**Where to find it.** Part of the [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md) family; every Mote recording can be
shared as a Mote Page.
