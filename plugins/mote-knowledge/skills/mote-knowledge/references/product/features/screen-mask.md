# Screen Mask

**Description.** Screen Mask helps a student focus while reading by cutting down
visual distraction. It offers three modes: a **mask** that dims the page around a
single line of text, a **ruler**, and an **underline**. The color can be set from a
range of dyslexia-friendly options, and the focus moves down the page with the
reader.

**How to use it.** Open the Mote Chrome Extension, go to **Reading Mode**, and select Screen
Mask. Choose the mode (mask, ruler, or underline) and a color, then move the
focus down the page with the arrow keys or the mouse as you read.

**Why it exists.** A full page of text overwhelms readers who lose their place or
get pulled around by everything competing for attention at once. Mote's Screen Mask
narrows reading to one line at a time over *any* page (webpage, Google Doc, or
digital reading material) and lets the student pick the form that suits them (mask,
ruler, or underline) and a dyslexia-friendly color, so the support fits the reader.

**Where to find it.** The [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md) (Chrome extension); works
on any webpage, Google Docs, and digital reading materials.
