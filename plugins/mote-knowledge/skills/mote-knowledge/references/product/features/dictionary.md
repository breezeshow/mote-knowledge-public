# Dictionary

**Description.** The Dictionary turns any word into a quick, multimodal
explanation. Click a word and a pop-up shows a clear image of its meaning alongside
the definition, which can be read aloud, or shown in the student's own language for
English Learners (English Language Learners). Any word looked up can be saved to a vocabulary list,
which stores it in [Mote Vocab](../mote-vocab.md) for later study.

**How to use it.** In the Mote Chrome Extension's **Reading Mode**, highlight a word and
click the Dictionary button. The pop-up shows the image and definition; click the
sound button to hear it read aloud, choose a language to see the definition in the
student's own language, or save the word to a vocabulary list.

**Why it exists.** Looking a word up usually means leaving the page for a
definition just as hard to decode as the original text. Mote's Dictionary is
multimodal: a modern, relatable image shows the meaning, the definition can be
heard aloud, and English Learners can read it in their own language. Every word
saved goes into Mote Vocab, so a quick lookup turns into lasting study. One click,
in the Mote Sidebar, no tab-switching.

**Where to find it.** The [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md) (Chrome extension).

**Good to know** *(surface when relevant).* The Dictionary also works inside
accessible PDFs viewed in Google Drive and other web-based PDF viewers.
