# Read Aloud

**Description.** Read Aloud turns any written content into natural, human-like
speech, including maths equations and words embedded in graphics or images. It
works on any webpage, and in Google Docs, Slides, PDFs, and Google Forms quizzes.
Each word is highlighted as it is read, so students can follow along. A diverse
range of voices is available, along with adjustable speed and highlight color.

**How to use it.** Open the Mote Chrome Extension and go to **Reading Mode**.
**Play Page** (the top button) reads the whole page aloud; **Select Area to
Read** (the second button down) lets you drag a box over any text, including text
inside images, PDFs, and scanned documents that can't normally be selected. You can
also simply highlight text on the page to hear just that part.

In Google Docs, **Play Page starts from the paragraph the cursor is in** rather
than returning to the top of the document. Click into the part you want to hear
and playback begins there.

**Why it exists.** Students can only learn from text they can access, and many
can't decode it fluently. Read Aloud removes that barrier, and does it better than
bolt-on alternatives: it follows the student across *any* webpage or document
instead of being tied to one site, the voices are genuinely human rather than
robotic, and speed and highlighting adjust to the individual, all from the Mote
Sidebar.

**Where to find it.** The [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md) (Chrome extension), and
inside [Mote PDF](../mote-pdf.md).

**Good to know** *(surface when relevant).* Read Aloud works on accessible PDFs,
not on every PDF, but a scanned or photocopied PDF can be made readable first:
[Mote PDF](../mote-pdf.md) runs OCR to accessibilize it, after which Read Aloud
works on it like any other document.
