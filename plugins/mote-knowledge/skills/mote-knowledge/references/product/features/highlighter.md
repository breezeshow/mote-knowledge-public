# Highlighter

> **Naming.** The **Highlighter** is the tool. **My Annotations** is the page where
> what it produces is kept. They are two things, not two names for one thing. See
> [`./my-annotations.md`](./my-annotations.md) for the page, and
> [`../../brand/terminology.md`](../../brand/terminology.md) for the rule.

**Description.** The Highlighter lets students mark important text in color on any
webpage or document. Four of the highlighter colors carry **editable labels**, so a
teacher or class can name them for specific reading strategies; two colors are fixed:
blue, labeled for vocabulary, and white, for clearing existing highlights. Everything
marked is saved to **[My Annotations](./my-annotations.md)**, grouped by the page it
came from, so a student can return to work they did earlier and see exactly what they
marked. Each highlight can carry a typed note (see
[Highlight Notes](./highlight-notes.md)). Highlighting with the vocabulary label also
sends the word to their Personal Vocabulary list in [My Vocab](../mote-vocab.md).

**How to use it.** Open the Mote Chrome Extension, go to **Reading Mode** in the Mote
Sidebar, and select the Highlighter. Choose a color, then click and drag over the text
you want to mark. To rename a label, pick one of the four central colors, hover over
its label, and click the pencil icon.

**Related features.**

- **[Highlight Notes](./highlight-notes.md):** a typed note attached to a highlight,
  saved with it in My Annotations. A highlight records that a passage mattered; the
  note records why. A distinct feature, not another name for the Highlighter.
- **Highlighter Labels:** the editable labels on the four central colors, which let a
  class match the Highlighter to its own reading and research strategies.
- **Export Highlights to Google Docs:** turns
  a page's highlights, plus the words looked up in the Dictionary, into a new Google
  Doc, laid out and including word illustrations where they exist.

**Why it exists.** Highlighting is a core study skill, but an ordinary highlight is
just color on a page, gone once the tab closes. Mote keeps every highlight on the My
Annotations page, so a student can go back to a page they worked on and see exactly
what they marked, and the editable labels let a class turn marking up text into a
deliberate reading strategy.

**Where to find it.** Reading Mode in the Mote Sidebar, in the
[Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md).
