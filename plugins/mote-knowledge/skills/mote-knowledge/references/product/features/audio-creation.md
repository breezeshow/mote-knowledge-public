# Audio Creation

**Description.** Mote's audio creation tools let students and teachers make voice
and audio content from the Chrome Extension. They are: **Voice Recording** (record your
voice); **Text to Audio** (turn written text into a downloadable, high-quality audio
file in natural voices and multiple languages); **Audio Upload** (share a
pre-recorded audio file); and **Dictate & Translate** (translate the transcript of a
recorded Mote into more than 60 languages). Recordings can be shared by link, QR
code, or embed code.

**How to use it.** Open the Mote Chrome Extension and go to **Creating Mode**, where the
audio creation tools live. Record audio, generate audio from text, or upload a
file, then share the result via link, QR code, or embed code.

**Why it exists.** Writing isn't the only way for a student to show what they know.
Mote's audio creation tools give every student a low-friction way to create and
communicate with voice (recording, generating audio from text, uploading existing
audio, and translating a recording's transcript) and everything made is instantly
shareable.

**Where to find it.** The [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md) (Chrome extension).
