# Online Audio Recorder

**Description.** The Online Audio Recorder is a browser-based recorder that needs
no extension installed. Record straight from the browser, and each recording gets an
automatic transcript carried within the voice note; share the result through a link
or QR code.

**How to use it.** Go to mote.com/record, set your transcript language, and start
recording. Share the recording with the generated link or QR code.

**Why it exists.** Not everyone can install a browser extension. Mote's Online
Audio Recorder needs nothing installed: it records in the browser at
mote.com/record, transcribes each recording automatically, and hands back a
shareable link or QR code, so creating and sharing audio is open to anyone, on any
device.

**Where to find it.** Part of the [Mote Chrome Extension](../chrome-extension.md) family, browser-based at
mote.com/record; no extension required.
