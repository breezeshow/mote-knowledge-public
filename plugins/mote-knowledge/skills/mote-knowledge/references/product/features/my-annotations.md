# My Annotations

> **Naming.** My Annotations is the **store**, not the tool. The tool that produces
> what it holds is the [Highlighter](./highlighter.md). See
> [`../../brand/terminology.md`](../../brand/terminology.md).

**Description.** My Annotations is the permanent store of everything a student marks
with the [Highlighter](./highlighter.md). Highlights are grouped by the page they came
from, each entry carrying the source link and the date it was made, and the whole set
is searchable. Any [Highlight Note](./highlight-notes.md) a student typed appears
alongside the highlight it belongs to.

This is what makes Mote's highlighting different from marking up a page in the
browser: the marks outlive the tab.

**Where to find it.** A **top-level item in the Mote dashboard side menu**, at
`app.mote.com/mastery/highlights`.

> The `/mastery/` path is a legacy URL from when the vocabulary product was called
> Mote Mastery. My Annotations is **not** part of [My Vocab](../mote-vocab.md) in the
> menu structure; it sits at the same level as My Vocab, My Classes, and My Activity.
> Do not describe it as living under My Vocab.

**Related.** Highlights marked with the **vocabulary** label also send that word to
the student's Personal Vocabulary list, which *is* part of
[My Vocab](../mote-vocab.md). That is the one genuine link between the two, and it
runs one way.
