# Mote Translator

**Description.** Mote Translator is Mote's live translation tool for multilingual
classrooms. A teacher starts a live stream from any device with a browser and a
microphone; as they speak, their words are transcribed and translated in real time,
and students open a shared link to follow along in their own language. It can
translate into several languages at once; students can choose text-only or
text-with-audio (read aloud in a human-like voice, highlighted as it plays); and
after the stream the full transcript and translations are available as a record.

**How to use it.** Go to `translator.mote.com`, click to start a stream, and share
the generated link or QR code with students. Students open the link and select
their language. After the stream, the transcript and translations are available from
the post-stream summary page.

**Why it exists.** Live, spoken instruction leaves multilingual learners behind in
a way written material doesn't; a lesson can't be paused to translate it.
Mote Translator removes that barrier in real time, with no setup beyond a link, so a
teacher can speak once and reach every student in their own language.

**Where to find it.** A separate, secondary product at `translator.mote.com`,
distinct from the Mote Chrome Extension. For most multilingual classroom needs the Chrome Extension's
translation tools are the better fit; Mote Translator is specifically for translating
live, spoken lessons.

**Good to know** *(surface when relevant).* Students get the most from
Mote Translator with headphones.
