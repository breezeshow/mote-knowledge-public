# Mote Translator

**Description.** Mote Translator is Mote's live translation tool for multilingual
classrooms. A teacher speaks, and students follow the lesson live in their own
language through a shared link or QR code. As the teacher speaks, their words are
transcribed and translated in real time; it can translate into several languages
at once; students can choose text-only or text-with-audio (read aloud in a
human-like voice, highlighted as it plays); and after the session the full
transcript and translations are available as a record.

**Conversation mode** is for talking with one person on one device, both ways,
for example a teacher and a parent.

**Who can use it.** Open to every teacher (adults only) since 5 October 2026.
Students can't start sessions. It is actively developed.

**How it's sold.** Separately from the Mote plans, as **prepaid hours**. No
subscription. Every teacher gets **1 free hour**. One hour of live translation
into one language uses 1 hour; 20 minutes into three languages also uses 1 hour.
An hour of Conversation mode uses 1 hour. Hours last 24 months, and a session is
never cut off mid-lesson.

- **Personal packs:** hours for your own sessions. Pay by card, use them in any
  session you start, buy more whenever you need them.
- **Group packs:** one pool for the whole organization; admins see each user's
  hours. Pay by card, invoice or purchase order.
- Prices are quoted in **US dollars only** for now.

**How to use it.** Go to `translator.mote.com`, start a session, and share the
generated link or QR code with students. Students open the link and select their
language. After the session, the transcript and translations are on the summary
page.

**Why it exists.** Live, spoken instruction leaves multilingual learners behind in
a way written material doesn't; a lesson can't be paused to translate it.
Mote Translator removes that barrier in real time, with no setup beyond a link, so a
teacher can speak once and reach every student in their own language.

**Where to find it.** A separate product with its own site at
`translator.mote.com`. mote.com keeps a short summary page that links out to it,
and the English Learner pages link to it too. It isn't part of the Mote plans,
and the homepage doesn't lead with it. For most multilingual classroom needs the
Chrome Extension's Translation is the better fit; Mote Translator is specifically
for live, spoken lessons and conversations.

**Good to know** *(surface when relevant).* Students get the most from
Mote Translator with headphones.
