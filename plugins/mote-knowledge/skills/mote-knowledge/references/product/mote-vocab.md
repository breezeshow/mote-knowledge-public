# My Vocab

> **Naming.** The product is **Mote Vocab**. **My Vocab** is what the customer
> sees: the page label in their dashboard. Use "Mote Vocab" when naming the
> product, "My Vocab" when describing where a user finds it. It was formerly
> **Mote Mastery**; that name is retired and should not appear in new material.

**Description.** My Vocab is Mote's set of vocabulary and study activities,
found on the My Vocab page. It includes:

- **Lists:** the collection of vocabulary lists a student or teacher has built, whether
  generated with the **Vocabulary Generator** or added to their **Personal Vocabulary**
  from the Chrome Extension. Personal Vocabulary fills up as a student clicks **"add to
  vocabulary list"** when looking a word up in the Dictionary. The **Vocabulary Generator**
  is opened from here: it builds word lists from a topic, a text passage, or an existing
  list, in English, French, or Spanish, with each word carrying a definition, an example,
  translations, and where relevant an illustration. Lists can be assigned to students or
  shared with colleagues. **Lists are editable:** remove a word that isn't wanted,
  rewrite a definition that doesn't fit the class, or add words the generator missed,
  giving the word, its part of speech, a definition, and an example of use.
- **Flash Cards:** study cards generated from a vocabulary list, including an
  **Image Mode** with picture-based cards.
- **Matching Quiz:** a straightforward activity where students match each word to its
  definition.
- **Sentence Star:** a timed activity where students write sentences using their
  vocabulary, with AI review giving immediate feedback.

**How to use it.** Open My Vocab either from the Chrome Extension (click the mode icon,
then My Vocab) or from the Mote dashboard, via the side menu. Open **Lists** to
generate or pick a vocabulary list, then practice with Flash Cards, Matching Quiz,
or Sentence Star. Teachers can assign lists to students or share them with colleagues.

**Why it exists.** Vocabulary is core to language acquisition, but a word list on
its own rarely sticks. My Vocab turns vocabulary into active practice (recall
with Flash Cards and Matching Quiz, construction with Sentence Star) so the words a
student collects while looking things up become material they genuinely learn.

**Where to find it.** The My Vocab page, reached from the Chrome Extension (mode icon →
My Vocab) or the Mote dashboard's side menu.

> **Not part of My Vocab.** [My Annotations](./features/my-annotations.md) is its own
> top-level item in the dashboard side menu, not one of My Vocab's activities. Its URL
> (`/mastery/highlights`) is a legacy path from when the product was called Mote
> Mastery, and does not imply it sits under My Vocab.
