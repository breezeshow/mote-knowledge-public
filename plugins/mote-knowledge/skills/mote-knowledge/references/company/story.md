# Company Story

Mote began with voice. In 2019, founders **Alex Nunes** and **Will Jackson** saw how
much a voice message could carry that typed text couldn't, and started building a
tool around it. They launched Mote in January 2020 and released the first Chrome
extension beta that March, just as schools around the world moved to remote
learning.

What began as a way for teachers to give richer feedback by voice grew through close
partnerships with school districts. Working alongside them, Mote kept running into
the same gap: the existing reading and writing accessibility tools didn't fit how
Chromebook-based K-12 classrooms actually worked. That is why Mote does what it
does today: it set out to build something designed for that context, a reading and
writing accessibility platform built around students, easy to use independently,
and there wherever they learn.
