# Mission, Vision & Values

**Mission.** Mote's mission is to give students who face barriers to learning
(those with learning disabilities, reading and writing challenges, and multilingual
learners) the tools to access learning independently. In doing so, it gives
teachers a real way to include every student in their classroom. Mote is, first and
foremost, a product for students.

**Vision.** Every K-12 student who needs reading, writing, or language support has
the Mote Chrome Extension with them, regardless of school size or specialist staffing.

**How Mote approaches its work.** Mote is a genuine company that cares about its
customers. It responds quickly, listens closely to what schools and teachers ask
for, and updates the product accordingly, and it believes strongly in innovation.
Mote works in partnership with its schools, building a product that helps students
genuinely thrive and learn. The tools are there to build skills and remove
barriers, never to let a student bypass the learning itself.
