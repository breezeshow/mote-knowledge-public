# Audiences

Mote is, first and foremost, a product for **students**, and it is sold mainly to
the **districts** that put it in their hands. This file maps who Mote serves to the
parts of Mote that help them, and how the audiences connect when a deal forms. The
feature files say *what a tool is*; this file says *who it is for*.

## Students Mote is built for

Mote is designed for students who face a barrier between themselves and their
learning. Three groups matter most.

**Students with learning disabilities,** including dyslexia, dysgraphia, and
attention-related needs, and students with IEPs or 504 plans. What helps:
[Read Aloud](../product/features/read-aloud.md) to access text by ear,
[Screen Mask](../product/features/screen-mask.md) and
[Focus Mode](../product/features/focus-mode.md) to cut visual load, and
[Text Prediction](../product/features/text-prediction.md) and
[Speech to Text](../product/features/speech-to-text.md) to get words onto the page.

**Students with reading and writing challenges,** students reading or writing below
grade level, without necessarily a formal diagnosis. What helps: Read Aloud to reach
text above their independent reading level,
[Mote Writing Review](../product/writing-review.md) to learn from their own
mistakes, Text Prediction, and the [Dictionary](../product/features/dictionary.md).

**Multilingual learners,** emerging bilinguals and English language learners. What
helps: [Webpage Translation](../product/features/webpage-translation.md) (which can
also read the translated text aloud), the Dictionary's native-language support, and
Read Aloud to connect written and spoken English.

## Teachers

Mote gives a teacher a realistic way to include every
student: because the support sits in the student's own hands, the teacher does not
have to differentiate every task by hand, or wait on scarce specialist staff.

## Administrators and districts: the buyer

Districts are Mote's buyer. A district leader weighs equitable access
at scale, alignment to [UDL](../education/udl.md) and [MTSS](../education/mtss.md),
privacy and compliance (FERPA, COPPA, GDPR), usage reporting, simple deployment, and
an affordable per-seat cost. [Mote Platform](../product/platform.md) is built
for exactly that.
