# Problems Mote Solves

This file names the problems Mote exists to solve: for students, for teachers, and
for the districts that have to deliver for both. It is the "why" behind the
[audiences](./audiences.md) and the product.

## For students

- **Grade-level text is out of reach.** A student who reads below grade level cannot
  access the material their class is built on, and falls further behind through no
  lack of ability to *understand* it. → Read Aloud lets them reach it by ear.
- **Ideas get stuck before the page.** A student who struggles to spell or
  physically write loses their thinking to the mechanics of getting it down. → Text
  Prediction and Speech to Text clear that path.
- **Language is a wall.** A multilingual learner can be capable in their first
  language but locked out of an English-only classroom. → Translation and the
  Dictionary let them take part now, while their English grows.
- **Correction doesn't teach.** A student who is simply corrected (or who
  one-click-accepts a fix) never learns why. → Mote Writing Review explains, and leaves
  the fix to the student.
- **The page itself overwhelms.** Dense, cluttered text defeats some students before
  they read a word. → Screen Mask and Focus Mode quieten it.
- **PDFs lock students out.** Worksheets and texts often arrive as PDFs, and most
  accessibility tools simply do not work on them. → Mote PDF makes a PDF fully
  workable.

## For teachers

- **One teacher cannot differentiate for thirty students by hand.** Real classes
  hold a wide spread of needs; preparing a separate version of every task for every
  student is not realistic.
- **Specialist staff are scarce.** Support that depends on a reading specialist or
  an aide cannot reach every student who needs it.

Mote answers both by putting the support in the *student's* own hands, so inclusion
no longer rests entirely on the teacher's time.

## For districts

- **Equitable access is required, and hard to deliver at scale.** IDEA, Section 504,
  and a UDL expectation all ask a district to make learning accessible to every
  student, but budgets and specialist staffing rarely stretch far enough.
- **Accommodations are uneven.** Without a common tool, what a student receives
  depends on which school, or which teacher, they happen to have.

Mote gives a district one affordable, consistent way to meet that obligation for
every student at once.
