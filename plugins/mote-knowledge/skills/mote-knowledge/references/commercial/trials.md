# Trials and pilots

Want evidence that Mote works before you commit? There are three ways in.

| Way | Who starts it | What you get | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Educator trial** | Any educator who signs up. Built for educators, but students who sign up themselves get it too | Everything in Unlimited, including one class | 14 days for new signups (30 days for accounts created before 1 October 2026) |
| **Class trial** | Any teacher with a Mote account | One class of up to 40 students (five classes on Unlimited). Each student gets the Unlimited tools | 90 days per student, from the day they join |
| **School or district pilot** | A school or district decision maker, with our team, through the pilot form | Terms set for the school or district: number of students, tools, length | Agreed per pilot |

Every pilot call to action links to the pilot form,
`justmote.typeform.com/to/zujXComF`, with `enquiry_type` set to the page the
link is on. The pricing page's free trial option and any "Ways to try Mote"
block show all three.

## How classes work

- The main way in is **Google Classroom**: the teacher imports the class and the
  students are in (the roster keeps syncing). A join link, a 6-letter class code
  or a QR code also works.
- On the trial or the Free plan a teacher can create **1 class**; on Unlimited,
  **5**. School and district plans have **no class limit**. Students can't
  create classes.
- Each class takes up to **40 students**. Each student gets the 90-day trial
  **once**, in their first class. Adults who join a class become co-teachers and
  get no student trial.
- Any teacher who creates a class gets Teacher Feature Controls and Assessment
  Mode for it. They stop having an effect when the class's trial ends.
- When a teacher's first student joins, the **teacher's own trial is extended to
  90 days in total** (once; not for paid teachers).
- After 90 days, students move to the **Free** plan, unless a school or district
  plan covers them. Their teacher sees "Free class trial ended on {date}".

**The most common support question**, and the answer:

> "Unlimited covers one person: you. Your students get 90 days of the full tools
> through your class. After that, they keep the Free tools. To keep every tool
> for your students, buy seats for them on a multi-seat plan, or ask your school
> or district about a plan."

## Other ways a trial is extended

- **Referrals:** +30 days per friend invited (up to 10); the invited friend gets
  a 60-day trial.
- **Mote Academy:** completing courses extends free access, and earning
  **Mote Certified Educator** (3 courses) unlocks a class trial for the teacher
  and 40 students. See [onboarding-and-learning.md](../product/onboarding-and-learning.md).

## When a trial ends

The user drops to the **Free** plan (see [plans.md](./plans.md)) unless a school
or district license covers them or they buy Unlimited.

## How to talk about it

- "Try Mote free" is fine for the trial.
- Never "Mote is free", "free for teachers" or "free for educators".
- Don't lead with the Free plan.
