# Pricing facts

> The authoritative, current source is
> **[mote.com/pricing](https://www.mote.com/pricing)**. Always cross-check
> before quoting a price externally.

## Headline prices

**Free.** $0 across all markets. Includes around 20 voice recordings a month
with a 1-minute cap, listening to and translating others' notes, and Read Aloud
in Google Forms where enabled.

**Unlimited (individual), per year**

| USD | GBP | EUR | AUD | CAD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $59.40 | £59.40 | €59.40 | A$71.40 | C$71.40 |

**Multi-seat, per seat, per year**

| USD | GBP | EUR | AUD | CAD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $49 | £49 | €49 | A$54 | C$54 |

Sold from **2 seats upward**; includes analytics and seat management. It is a
volume plan, not a schools-only plan; a staff group or a student cohort of any
size above one qualifies. On the pricing page this tab is labelled
**Multi-seat**; the underlying product name is **Mote Student or Staff Groups**.

**School (building), per year**

| USD | GBP | EUR | AUD | CAD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $1,500 | £1,400 | €1,400 | A$2,000 | C$2,100 |

Covers **up to 1,000 seats** in one building.

**District.** Per-seat, quote-based in all markets; rate depends on seats.
Published as **Mote District or Multi-site**.
