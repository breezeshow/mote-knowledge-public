# Plans

The authoritative prices are in [pricing-facts.md](./pricing-facts.md); this
file gives the practical shape so an agent or salesperson can route a
conversation correctly. The plan names below are exactly as the pricing page
shows them.

## Free

**Includes:** Dictionary, Screen Mask, Focus Mode, Audio Generation, Audio
Upload, Mote PDF. 20 voice notes a month, up to 1 minute each, including in
Google Workspace. One class. Read Aloud inside a Google Form a subscribed teacher
shares, with no subscription needed per student.

**Leaves out:** Read Aloud anywhere else, Translation, Highlighter, Text
Prediction, Speech to Text, Mote Writing Review, Mote Vocab, longer voice notes.

How to describe it: *"With a subscription, a teacher can turn on Read Aloud in a
Google Form and share it, and every student can use it without their own
subscription. Students on the Free plan don't get Read Aloud anywhere else."*

## Unlimited

For **one user, a teacher or a student**. Bought online (Chargebee checkout),
monthly or yearly, and can be gifted.

**Includes:** every Mote Sidebar tool (Read Aloud with human-like voices,
Translation in 60+ languages, Speech to Text, Text Prediction, Highlighter,
Screen Mask, Dictionary, Mote Writing Review), Mote PDF, Mote Vocab, Google
Forms (turning on Read Aloud for a Form students use) and the other Google
integrations, voice notes up to 5 minutes with Motebook, and **up to 5
classes**, each giving up to 40 students a 90-day trial.

**Leaves out:** admin features (seat management, adding users, domain
enablement, analytics, ClassLink sign-in).

Customers often call Unlimited "premium". Use that word once in an FAQ
("Unlimited, sometimes called premium"), never as a plan name.

## Student or staff groups (the Multi-seat tab)

Unlimited for a group of 2 or more people, priced per seat on a sliding scale.
Self-serve online for 2 to 100 seats ("Buy N seats"); larger groups go through
sales. A volume plan, not a schools-only plan: a staff group or a student cohort
of any size above one qualifies.

## School building

Every tool for every staff member and student in one building (up to 1,000), no
class limit, plus the admin layer (below). Bought by quote.

## District or multi-site

The same for a whole district, from $2,500 a year. Sales-led ("Talk to sales").
Say "District plans start at $2,500 a year. Contact us for a quote." Never
advertise a per-student or per-seat district rate.

### What school and district plans add

- **Rollout:** IT installs from the Google Admin console. Admins add people by
  domain, CSV upload or email. Google, ClassLink or email sign-in; ClassLink
  rostering.
- **Controls:** Feature Access (each tool On, Off for all, or Off by default),
  privacy controls, Assessment Mode.
- **Analytics:** the My School dashboard, usage data and the Impact Report.
- **Support:** dedicated support and PD options.

See [Mote Platform](../product/platform.md) for the detail.

Mote does **not** offer OneRoster, Clever, SAML or Microsoft sign-in.
