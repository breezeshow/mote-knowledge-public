# Plans

Mote offers four tiers. The authoritative, current source is
[mote.com/pricing](https://www.mote.com/pricing); this file gives the practical
shape so an agent or salesperson can route a conversation correctly.

## Free

For anyone who wants to try Mote or use it lightly. Includes basic voice
recording (around 20 recordings a month with a 1-minute cap), the ability to
listen to and translate others' notes, and Read Aloud in Google Forms where
enabled.

## Unlimited (individual)

For an individual teacher or user. Includes the full
[Chrome Extension toolkit](../product/chrome-extension.md): Read Aloud with human-like voices,
Speech to Text, Translation in 60+ languages, Mote Writing Review, Text Prediction,
Highlighter, Screen Mask, Dictionary, plus voice recording (with a 5-minute
cap) and the ability to save and reuse recordings in Motebook. Includes
1 class for student monitoring.

## Multi-Seat (schools)

For institutions. Adds user management: adding users by email or CSV, multiple
classes, domain enablement, and usage analytics. Pricing is quote-based.

## Custom (districts)

For district-wide deployment. The full platform with district admin controls,
unlimited users, OneRoster sync, and the
[Impact Report](../product/platform.md). Pricing is quote-based.
