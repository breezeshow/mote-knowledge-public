# Outcomes

Published research on Mote's impact. Two formal studies exist; both pre-date
Mote's evolution into the broader accessibility platform and focus on the
voice-feedback workflow specifically.

## Published research

- **LEO Academy Trust PedTech Impact Report.** A study led by Dr. Fiona
  Aubrey-Smith examining Mote's role in LEO Academy Trust's 1:1 device
  ecosystem and feedback practice across its primary schools (ages 5-11), as
  part of the trust's broader PedTech toolbox.
  Read: [leoacademytrust.co.uk/2801/pedtech-impact-report](https://www.leoacademytrust.co.uk/2801/pedtech-impact-report).
- **"Unlocking Potential: The Transformative Power of Voice Feedback in Education"**
  by John M. Weathers, Ph.D. A study across schools in the US, UK, and Australia
  finding that teachers using Mote for voice feedback gave feedback more
  frequently and in greater detail, and perceived a significant improvement in
  students' engagement and outcomes.
  Read: [mote.com/research-studies/unlocking-potential-the-transformative-power-of-voice-feedback-in-education](https://www.mote.com/research-studies/unlocking-potential-the-transformative-power-of-voice-feedback-in-education).
  PDF: [mote.com/docs/Educational_impact_of_Mote__by_John_Weathers_PhD.pdf](https://www.mote.com/docs/Educational_impact_of_Mote__by_John_Weathers_PhD.pdf).
