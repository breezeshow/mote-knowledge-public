# Case Studies

Mote's published case studies. Use these for the proof point in an RFP, a
sales deck, or a blog post. Each lives at `mote.com/case-studies/<slug>`.

## Multilingual & language learning

- **[Greece Central School District](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/greece-central-school-district-new-york)** *(New York):* large suburban district serving families across 70+ languages; Mote for language access in classrooms.
- **[New Canaan Public Schools](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/how-new-canaan-public-schools-used-mote-to-revamp-world-languages)** *(Connecticut):* World Languages department.
- **[Tri-County Regional Center for Education](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/increasing-language-learning-opportunities-for-all-students)** *(Nova Scotia, Canada):* accessible and equitable language learning.
- **[Bialik Hebrew Day School](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/four-languages-one-voice)** *(Toronto):* instruction in four languages (English, French, Hebrew, Yiddish).

## Feedback and teacher workflow

- **[LEO Academy Trust](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/how-leo-academy-trust-uses-mote-to-achieve-its-ambitious-feedback-policy-objectives)** *(England):* multi-academy trust of 9 primary schools; Mote in support of an ambitious feedback policy.
- **[City of Wolverhampton College](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/city-of-wolverhampton-college-and-mote-for-21st-century-feedback)** *(England):* further-education college.

## District / cross-curricular adoption

- **[Hauppauge UFSD](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/hauppauge-k-12)** *(Long Island, NY):* easy, accessible, collaborative use across the district.
- **[Irvington Union Free School District](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/simple-intuitive-and-easy-to-get-started)** *(New York):* simple, intuitive, easy to get started.
- **[East Stroudsburg Area School District](https://www.mote.com/case-studies/bridging-the-digital-divide-at-esasd)** *(Pennsylvania):* Mote in support of digital literacy and the teacher-student connection.
