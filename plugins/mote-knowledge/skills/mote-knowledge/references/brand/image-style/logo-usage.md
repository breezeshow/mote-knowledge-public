# Logo usage

## Variants

| Variant | File | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary lockup (dark)** | `mote-lockup-dark.png` | Warm-white, white, or light backgrounds. Default branding choice. |
| **Light / mid lockup** | `mote-lockup-light.png` | Medium-tone backgrounds where the primary lockup loses contrast. |
| **White / inverted lockup** | `mote-lockup-white.png` | Dark backgrounds (dark purple, dark gray, photography). |
| **M mark (standalone)** | `mote-mark.png` | **Design purposes inside images, not for branding.** See "Lockup vs M mark" below. |

Canonical lockup URLs live at `https://media.mote.com/marketing/brand-assets/`.

## Lockup vs M mark

**The lockup is the default for branding.** Almost every Mote-facing
surface (landing pages, footers, slide decks, email headers, social
profiles, app headers) uses the full lockup. If the question is *"where
does the Mote brand mark go on this surface?"*, the answer is the lockup.

**The M mark is for design purposes inside images.** It's a circular
purple-and-white M designed to work as a decorative or compositional
element *inside* a larger image, for example as a small mark on a hero
illustration, an accent inside a marketing graphic, or a sticker-style
placement on a piece of design work. It is **not** used as the primary
branding signature on any Mote surface.

**If in doubt, use the lockup.**

## Variant selection rule (lockups only)

Choose by **background contrast**, not by surface color name:

- **Light background** (warm-white, white, light tints) → **Primary
  (dark)**.
- **Mid-tone background** (medium gray, warm beige, mid-saturation Mote
  family tints) → **Light / mid**.
- **Dark background** (dark purple, dark gray, photography, video) →
  **White / inverted**.

Test by placing the lockup against the background and squinting. If the
lockup competes with the background or loses definition, switch variants.

## Layering

**The logo (lockup or M mark) always sits on the top layer** of the
composition. Above any screenshot, illustration, blob, photo, or
background element. Nothing crosses over the logo.

## Clear space (lockup)

Minimum clear space around the lockup is the **height of the "M" in the
Mote wordmark**, on all four sides. Nothing should encroach: no text, no
graphics, no edge of the artboard.

## Minimum size (lockup)

| Surface | Minimum width |
|---|---|
| **Digital** (web, app, slide deck) | 80px |
| **Print** | 0.75 inches (19mm) |

Below these sizes the lockup loses clarity, but in those cases the M
mark is *not* the right substitute for branding. The M mark is for design,
not signature.

## Do / don't

**Do**

- Use the primary (dark) lockup on a warm-white hero background.
- Use the white lockup on a dark-purple footer.
- Use the M mark as a decorative element inside an illustration or
  marketing graphic.
- Keep the logo (lockup or M mark) on the top layer of the composition.
- Respect the clear space; keep adjacent text and graphics at least
  one "M-height" away from the lockup.
- Use the exact PNG files from `brand/assets/` or
  `media.mote.com/marketing/brand-assets/`.

**Don't**

- Use the M mark as the primary brand signature on a page (use the
  lockup).
- Stretch the lockup or M mark horizontally or vertically (preserve
  aspect ratio).
- Change the lockup or M mark color.
- Add effects (drop shadow, gradient overlay, glow, outline).
- Place the lockup on a competing-saturation background (Mote Purple
  lockup on a bright purple background).
- Use a low-resolution or rasterised lockup at large sizes (use the
  full-res PNG).
- Reproduce the lockup or M mark from memory or redraw it.
- Put anything over the top of the logo.

## Notes / open items

- **Logo on photography**: when placing the white lockup on photography,
  consider a soft dark overlay or position over the darkest area of the
  photo to maintain contrast.
- **Animated logo**: are there approved logo animations (e.g. for video
  intros)? If yes, document timing and motion rules.
